    Discover jobs on LinkedIn

    Runs LinkedIn searches built from your preferences, opens each new listing, judges how well it fits you, and queues the good ones.

    Your LinkedIn account, your risk. LinkedIn's terms don't allow automated browsing, and LinkedIn can restrict accounts it thinks are automated. Discover paces itself like a person, stops after a set number of listings, and never clicks Apply or Easy Apply, but the risk isn't zero.

    Runs your searches in a LinkedIn tab of its own and opens each new listing to judge it. Good fits go to the Apply queue. Each press carries on where the last one stopped; searches start again from the newest listings the next day. Keep the LinkedIn tab in front while it runs (use another window meanwhile).
